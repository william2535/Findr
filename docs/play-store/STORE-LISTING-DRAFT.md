**Full description:**

FINDR is a practical toolkit for fire and security engineers and learners.

Save equipment manuals for site visits, find source-backed information, practise supported panel menus, and keep a clear fault-finding record of observations and readings. Create Panel Pals for your selected equipment and return to saved conversations when you need them.

Includes:

- Manual archive search, local PDF import and saved document viewing.
- Panel emulators, menu maps and hints for supported models.
- Fault logs, readings, handover records and exports.
- Engineering calculators, reference tools and revision quizzes.
- Local manual search and supported on-device AI after required resources are installed.
- Optional online AI using your own supported provider API account and explicit consent. A connection and provider charges may apply.

Offline availability depends on the manuals and resources you have prepared. Local AI capability varies by device. FINDR shows source references and uncertainty; it cannot confirm the installed device or repair from a configuration file alone.

FINDR is an independent student-built project. It does not connect to or control real panels, provide manufacturer approval, or replace the applicable manual, training and site safety procedures.
