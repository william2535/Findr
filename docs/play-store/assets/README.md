# FINDR Google Play artwork

Prepared 18 November 2026 for the separate `com.findr.play` listing. The original website, Android preview and uploaded internal draft binary are unchanged.

| Console field | File | Verified format |
| --- | --- | --- |
| App icon | [play-listing-icon-512.png](play-listing-icon-512.png) | 512 × 512, 32-bit RGBA PNG, 173,049 bytes |
| Feature graphic | [play-feature-graphic-1024x500.png](play-feature-graphic-1024x500.png) | 1024 × 500, 24-bit RGB PNG, 536,406 bytes, no alpha |

The icon preserves the approved Manual Hunt book-and-magnifier design, with a clean square yellow background and no baked-in corner mask. The feature graphic uses FINDR's black/yellow branding, the existing “Ready for the job” wording and a promotional illustration of manuals/search. It does not claim to show an actual app screen, a manufacturer-approved procedure or tested performance.

Alt text:

- Icon: “FINDR Manual Hunt icon: a black open manual with a yellow magnifying glass.”
- Feature: “FINDR: Ready for the job. Manuals, Panel Pals and engineer tools, with yellow search artwork.”

## On iPhone

Download [FINDR-Play-Store-Pack.zip](FINDR-Play-Store-Pack.zip), save it in Files and tap it to extract. In Play Console's Main store listing, choose **Add assets → Choose Files** under each matching field, then select the corresponding PNG. The ZIP itself is not an image upload. The pack also includes the current privacy policy and these instructions.

Privacy policy URL to paste into the Console field:

https://github.com/william2535/Findr/blob/release/play-store/docs/play-store/PRIVACY-POLICY.md

FINDR is free to download and use. Optional external AI providers may charge under their own terms. The artwork deliberately has no pricing badge; Google discourages price claims in these image fields. Set the app price to Free in Play Console; this asset preparation does not change its pricing setting.

## Validation and scope

PNG signatures, IHDR dimensions, 8-bit channels, color types, byte sizes, full decoding and archive contents were checked. Both upload images were visually inspected at their final dimensions for legibility, edge quality, clipping and exact text. The images meet the documented file specifications; this is not a guarantee of Google review approval.

Image generation produced the artwork; deterministic resampling/export only fitted Google's exact pixel sizes and formats. Original generated masters are retained in the development workspace. No app code, service worker, live deployment, uploaded AAB or signing credentials are included in this pack.

Official reference: [Google Play preview asset requirements](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9866151?hl=en), checked 18 November 2026. Actual app screenshots remain a separate requirement and are not fabricated by this artwork pack.
