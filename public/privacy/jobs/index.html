Jobs Privacy Policy

Effective date: 18 November 2026
App: Jobs for Android, version 1.0.0
Package: com.willflood.jobs
Developer: Will Flood
Privacy and support: Williamflood8@gmail.com

Your Jobs workspace is stored on your device. You choose when to export, print or share it. The full policy below explains local storage, separate service providers and how to delete your information.

1. About this policy

Jobs is a workspace for recording site visits, plans, equipment, notes, photographs and work records. This policy explains how the Android app handles information and how to contact Will Flood about privacy. It covers the Android edition identified above. The Jobs website and other apps have separate storage; their records do not automatically appear in this app.

2. Your workspace stays on your device

Jobs stores and processes your workspace locally on your Android device. It does not require a Jobs account and has no automatic cloud synchronisation or developer-hosted database of your jobs.

This Android release contains its application files and tools. It does not request Internet permission or automatically upload your workspace to Will Flood, an analytics service or an AI service. It includes no advertising, behavioural analytics or in-app crash-reporting SDK. The included calculators and learning tools run locally.

Local processing still involves personal information when you enter or import it. “Stored locally” does not mean that Jobs stores no personal data.

3. Information you choose to store

Depending on how you use Jobs, your local records may contain:

  • Site and customer names, addresses, contact names, telephone numbers and email addresses.
  • Engineer names, appointments, timesheets, work descriptions and typed acknowledgements.
  • Access instructions, plans, equipment details, test results, defects, maintenance answers and service records.
  • Notes, captions, photographs, imported drawings and other information contained in the files you select.
  • Product, supplier, purchase-order and cost information that you record.
  • Previous drawing revisions, earlier evidence, submission history, saved quick defects, and local calculator inputs or learning progress.

Jobs uses this information to display and edit your work, preserve its history, calculate local results, prepare reports, and create or restore files you request. It does not automatically send purchase orders to suppliers or timesheets to an employer.

Imported files and photographs can contain information beyond what is visible on screen, including original drawing content or embedded image metadata. Jobs is not an anonymisation tool and does not promise to remove that information. Include only information you are authorised to use.

4. Files, photographs and device access

When you choose an import or add a picture, Jobs opens Android's file picker. The app reads the files you select and stores the content needed for your records. It does not scan your whole device or automatically upload your photo library.

This release does not request access to your address book, device location, microphone or camera. Contact details and site locations are information you type or import, rather than information obtained from those device permissions.

5. Exports, printing and other services

You control when information leaves the private workspace:

  • Export job, Export workspace backup, timesheet exports and drawing exports create files containing the selected records. Complete backups and job packages can include photographs, original drawings, internal notes and retained history that are not all shown in a printed report.
  • Android's save dialog lets you choose a destination. If you choose a cloud document provider, that provider may upload and store the file under its own privacy policy. Jobs does not choose a cloud destination for you.
  • Print report passes the prepared report to Android's printing system. Your selected print service, printer or PDF destination then handles the report.
  • Opening Zone Sketch, FINDR, Google Play or a reference link opens a separate app or website. That service may receive ordinary connection information, such as your IP address, and applies its own privacy policy. Opening a link does not automatically transfer your Jobs workspace.

In this Android release, transferring a drawing to Zone Sketch uses the manual export, edit and import workflow. The Panel Pal context feature creates a file; there is no live FINDR job adapter or cloud AI connection in Jobs. If you later send or import that file into another service, that is a separate transfer under your control.

Google Play, Android and your chosen service providers may process installation, update or diagnostic information under their own policies. That is separate from the local Jobs workspace.

6. Storage and security

Jobs keeps its databases, preferences and unfinished export copies within the app's Android storage. The app is configured to exclude this storage from Android's automatic cloud backup and device-transfer backup. Use Export workspace backup if you need a recoverable copy.

Jobs has no separate account password, app lock or encrypted local vault. Anyone able to unlock your device and open Jobs may be able to read its saved records. Use your device's security controls.

Exported JSON files, workspace backups and reports are not password-protected or encrypted by Jobs. Keep them in an appropriate protected location and share them only with your intended recipients. The protection of files outside Jobs depends on the storage or service you choose.

7. Retention, history and deletion

Jobs does not automatically expire your saved workspace. Some edits preserve earlier versions, and moving a timesheet session to trash retains its history. Removing information from a current view does not necessarily remove it from earlier records or exports.

To remove all of this app's local records, use Android Settings → Apps → Jobs → Storage → Clear storage/Clear data, or uninstall Jobs. Menu names vary by device. Export anything you want to keep before doing this. Clearing only the cache is not the same as clearing the saved workspace.

These actions do not delete original imported files, exported backups, PDFs, printed copies, files in cloud storage, or copies held by other people. Delete those separately where they are stored. The Jobs website also has its own separate browser storage.

During an export, Jobs prepares a private temporary copy. Incomplete temporary copies are cleaned up when the app next starts. A completed export interrupted before saving may remain for recovery until you save or explicitly discard it. Clearing the app's storage also removes these private recovery copies.

Will Flood cannot remotely access, recover or delete a workspace that exists only on your device.

8. Contacting support

If you email Williamflood8@gmail.com, Will Flood receives your email address, message and any attachments you choose to send. This information is used to answer your enquiry, investigate the issue and handle privacy requests. Sending an email is optional. Avoid sending a complete customer workspace when a description or a redacted example will do.

For support correspondence, Will Flood relies on the legitimate interest in responding to enquiries and maintaining the app. Information needed to fulfil a legal obligation, including handling applicable data-protection requests, is used for that obligation. Support correspondence is retained only while needed to deal with the enquiry and any necessary follow-up, dispute or legal obligation, then deleted when no longer needed. You may ask for earlier deletion.

Email is handled through Google Gmail and the providers used to send it. Those providers may process messages outside the UK. Google's privacy policy explains its processing and international-transfer arrangements: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Your support information is not sold or used for advertising. Jobs does not use your records to train an AI model or make automated decisions about you with legal or similarly significant effects.

9. Your choices and rights

You decide what to enter, import, export and share. You can review and correct current records in the app and export a copy of your saved work. For records about other people, you or your organisation are responsible for deciding why the information is needed and handling any requests about those records.

For personal information held by Will Flood, such as support correspondence, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction or portability where applicable. You may also object to processing based on legitimate interests. Contact Williamflood8@gmail.com with your request; you do not need to send your workspace to ask a privacy question.

You can raise a concern with the UK Information Commissioner's Office: https://ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint/

10. Changes to this policy

If Jobs changes how it handles information, this policy will be updated and its effective date changed. Changes that introduce a new use or transfer of your information will be explained before that processing begins.

Questions about this policy? Email Williamflood8@gmail.com. This policy is also available offline in the Android app under About / Help & data → Privacy policy.
