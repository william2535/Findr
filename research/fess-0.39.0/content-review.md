# FESS question bank: coverage and review

The supplied Fire & Security Exam Revision Guide, version 5.01 issued 4 January 2024, contains 196 numbered revision points on PDF positions 7–24. All 196 are represented in `public/app/data/fess-mocks.json`, with their original question number and PDF position. The source PDF and working extraction are not distributed with the app or GitHub backup. Public reference documents are linked, not republished.

The practice wording supplies a clear scope and four explained choices. It does not claim to reproduce a live examination or predict its grade. Twenty sets cover every point once: nineteen sets of ten and one of six. First-attempt scores are preserved independently from completion after corrections. A separate subject option groups the same 196 points into 31 quizzes across ten disciplines/topics, each with four to eight questions and an approximate five-minute estimate; no countdown forces the learner to rush. Understanding is the learner’s explicit self-assessment, never inferred from a correct or corrected answer.

## Review decisions

- Keep valid underlying answers and give worked arithmetic where useful. For example, 4 Ω in parallel with 1 Ω gives 0.8 Ω; two 2.2 kΩ resistors in parallel give 1.1 kΩ; 270 Ω, 470 Ω and 550 Ω in parallel give approximately 130.73 Ω. Correct choices are distributed across A–D.
- Use explicit manufacturer, configuration, legal jurisdiction and document edition where they affect the answer. General cable, fuse, standby-duration and safety assumptions are not presented as universal installation instructions.
- Display a revision note where an older statement is incomplete or contradicted by checked guidance. Examples include ACOP meaning, UK nominal supply voltage, access-control standards, the different roles of IP ratings and fire-resistant cable, MCP height tolerance, servicing intervals, handover records and escape-release design.
- Keep wall-VAD mounting height distinct from a product coverage rating. Keep sensing-element distance from ceilings distinct from floor-to-device height. Do not turn detector radius into a universal circular floor-area allowance.
- Battery and intruder-grade questions establish the applicable grade, supply, signalling and documented conditions. A quoted assessment-kit rule is labelled with that scope and is not a complete installed-system design.
- Respect hearing-loss and communication needs without assuming lip-reading, eye-contact or other behaviour determines ability or intent.
- Keep the 2024 guide’s assessment format separate from current resource editions and from the learner’s enrolled version. FINDR’s correction-to-100% flow is a learning exercise, not the official Highfield pass or distinction calculation.

## Sources and limits

Each question contains source identifiers resolving to a public title, HTTPS link, section and, where verified, PDF position and edition. The bank links current Highfield and Skills England resources alongside FIA, Hochiki, NSI, HSE, ICO, GOV.UK, manufacturer and calculation references. See `current-guides.md` for the current-resource review and assessment-version distinctions.

The research checked public guidance and manufacturer material. It did not obtain full paywalled British Standards, verify a particular learner’s enrolled EPA, or establish that this is an official/current live question bank. Public links can change; the saved question, explanations and cited edition remain in historical practice runs.
