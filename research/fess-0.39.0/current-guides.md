# Current public FESS revision resources — checked 12 November 2026

## What applies to the apprentice

The FESS apprenticeship reference is **ST0189**, not ST0310 (ST0310 is Associate Project Manager). The official Skills England page for ST0189 v1.2 is accessible and lists these start windows:

- v1.0: 19 August 2016 to 29 February 2024.
- v1.1: 1 March 2024 to 7 December 2025. Occupational standard, EPA plan and funding band revised.
- v1.2: 8 December 2025 onward; the change is labelled **Updated SSA**.

A learner finishing in 2026 can still be enrolled against an earlier version. The app must ask the learner to check their enrolled standard/EPA version with their provider, rather than imply the newest paper applies to everyone. The uploaded guide is dated 4 January 2024 and predates the v1.1 start window.

Official source, HTTP 200:
https://skillsengland.education.gov.uk/apprenticeships/st0189-v1-2

Highfield currently presents separate v1.0, v1.1 and v1.2 resources. Its latest v1.2 kit is v5.0; the other research task verified the 60-question, 75-minute knowledge test, 36 pass / 48 distinction thresholds and M-EPA-FESST3002 sample dated 20 May 2025. These are **official test format facts**, not the rules of FINDR's short correction-to-100% learning mode. Do not describe the uploaded older 90-minute / 40 / 50 guide as universally wrong for every cohort.

https://www.highfieldassessment.com/apprenticeship-standards/construction/fire-emergency-and-security-systems-technician

## Other public guides actually inspected

### FIA: changes to BS 5839-1:2025

The FIA release article is dated **20 June 2025**. Its linked PDF is publicly accessible (HTTP 200; 530,726 bytes), **Version 1, June 2025**. The standard came into effect on 30 April 2025, replacing the 2017 edition, according to the FIA's 14 October 2025 explanatory article.

https://www.fia.uk.com/news/fia-launches-bs-5839-1-2025-guidance-note.html
https://www.fia.uk.com/static/77c28e9f-486e-4b96-a82e4bb37f4f3882/Guide-to-the-changes-in-BS-5839-1-2025.pdf

Useful verified points in the actual PDF:

- PDF page 3: 2025 clause 21.2.4 retains smoke sensing elements 25–600 mm and heat sensing elements 25–150 mm below ceilings, with the stated exceptions. Clause numbers changed; the basic distances did not.
- PDF page 13: handover documentation includes a cause-and-effect matrix or textual description, appropriate to the system.
- PDF page 14: successive servicing is approximately six months, with the described 5–7-month tolerance and acceptance-date datum. Do not present the old absolute six-month maximum as the complete current wording.
- PDF page 15: if effective repair cannot be completed, fault indications must not be concealed. Remote services/cybersecurity and modification documentation have explicit treatment.

This is a technical change guide, not the entire paid British Standard. It does not establish every possible installation or examination answer.

### FIA: zone plans and categories

The zone-plan article is dated **29 May 2025**, explicitly aligned with BS 5839-1:2025. It describes a zone plan's purpose, correct orientation, location next to CIE and the consequences of absent/inaccurate plans.

https://www.fia.uk.com/news/updated-fia-guidance-fire-detection-and-alarm-zone-plans-now-aligned-with-bs-5839-1-2025.html

The categories article is dated **23 December 2025** and links a currently accessible Version 2 PDF. The PDF footer has an inconsistent date string, “January 2026 2025”; record Version 2 and 2025-standard alignment without inventing a precise release date. The article explains categories M, L1–L5 and P1–P2, and selection responsibility. The PDF's quoted/reproduced material is not to be copied wholesale into FINDR.

https://www.fia.uk.com/news/new-fia-guidance-on-bs-5839-1-fire-alarm-categories.html
https://www.fia.uk.com/static/0a7e11ea-ab5d-46b1-af30d8e32b25cc5a/FIA-Guidance-Fire-Alarm-Categories-Final.pdf

### Hochiki: BS 5839-1:2025 pocket guide

Actual manufacturer PDF retrieved and inspected by the question-authoring team. It is a useful free current overview, not a replacement for the full standard.

https://assets.hochikiglobal.com/assetbank-hochikieurope/assetfile/16136.pdf

Important scope distinctions: the manual-call-point drawing gives nominal 1.4 m with +200/-300 mm tolerance. The VAD section distinguishes a wall installation minimum of 2.1 m from a W-2.4-5 product's rated coverage/mounting-height classification; do not silently substitute 2.4 m for every minimum mounting-height question. The smoke-detector 7.5 m coverage distance is not a universal circular floor-area allowance. The content team is applying these scopes to the matching uploaded questions.

### NSI: access control and safe escape

NSI's article dated **5 June 2025** confirms **NCP 109 Issue 4** and withdrawal of Issue 3 on **30 September 2025**. The public safe-escape explanation describes performance-based building, fire and product requirements, rather than prescribing one universal door-release arrangement. BS 7273-4, EN 179, EN 1125 and EN 13637 are discussed in their appropriate roles.

https://www.nsi.org.uk/nsi-code-of-practice-ncp-109-issue-4-supporting-nsi-installers-with-guidance-on-the-latest-access-control-system-standards/
https://www.nsi.org.uk/access-control-safe-escape-and-ncp-109-issue-4/

These support correcting the uploaded “always disconnect the positive wire” claim into a question about the documented escape strategy and suitable equipment. The public pages do not establish a universal Grade 3 biometric reader requirement or a universal Grade 3 battery duration.

### ECS/JIB: free FESS Systems Operative sample guides — a different assessment

The current ECS assessment page links free general, Fire, Fire and Emergency Lighting, Security, and Combined Fire/Security guides. The general PDF is actually dated **08-2022**, despite being linked today. It describes a **30-question, 30-minute, 24-correct pass** assessment. This is the FESS Systems Operative/ECS route, **not** the Level 3 apprenticeship EPA; do not mix its format or thresholds with Highfield.

The current page explicitly says there is **no database of questions to revise from** for that assessment, and that the samples support training. The combined guide contains sample questions without supplied answers, inviting research/discussion. No newer edition date was established in that sample file. Link it as a currently linked supplementary sample, not a newly published 2026 guide or complete answer bank.

## Limits and content handling

All named public pages/PDFs above were fetched successfully; source date/version was read where stated. Search results alone were not treated as source evidence. A current link does not make an old document a new edition. Full BS/EN standards may require licensed access; this work did not obtain or reproduce paywalled full standards. The sources are linked for revision and factual checking; their papers/artwork were not republished. FINDR's question bank is based on the user-supplied revision topics, with scoped wording and visible revision notes wherever the older answer is incomplete or contradicted by checked guidance.

Downloaded reference PDFs/text remain under `/tmp/fess-current-guides/` for this research and are not part of the app source export.
