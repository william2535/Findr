Asset provenance and rendering

XFP front fascia and instructions are original colour PDF vector figures
rendered at 3x using PyMuPDF. Exact PDF page numbers and clipping rectangles
in PDF points are recorded in face-asset-clips.json. Live LCD content,
indicator states and transparent button hit areas are overlaid. Original
printed example LCD text and lit lamps are covered completely by runtime
state. The cabinet outline and finish use CSS derived from the corresponding
photograph; the plastic and metal cabinet layouts remain distinct. A small
C-TEC/XFP brand mark supplements the original manual figure to reflect the
pictured product. No fictional controls are added to either fascia.

The TWINFLEXpro² front figure is the original embedded PNG from Rev18 p21,
PDF image xref76, 689x1244. The Write Enable detail is the original embedded
JPEG from p19, xref66, 300x163. They are copied without image generation or
invented component detail. The interactive front uses HTML/CSS at positions
measured against the p21 figure. The blue upper fascia, red alarm buttons,
cream/gray cabinet, right-hand seam and corner fasteners follow the photo.
The lower system-indication strip is charcoal with light lettering and
pale inactive indicator windows, as visible through the closed keypad flap
in that photograph; its colour is not inferred from the monochrome diagram.
The hidden programming keypad is rendered with neutral gray controls; its
colour cannot be verified from the closed-flap photograph. Its button names,
order, positions and numeric/letter legends follow the open-flap manual
figure. This is a flat interactive training illustration, not a photograph.
The original picture and original open-flap manual figure are both available
through the in-app comparison option.

Specific distinctions preserved

- XFP 16 has a left instruction column, central compact arrow diamond,
  2x8 zone bank and an access key in the same fascia.
- XFP 32 has a left control cluster, right two-line LCD and 2x16 zone bank,
  with the access key in the separate lower instruction strip.
- XFP keeps Up/1, Accept/2, Down/3, Escape/4, Menu, More information,
  Silence internal sounder, Control panel reset, Investigate and
  Silence/resound sounders. Access uses the existing code/menu flow;
  opening the cover is not represented as entering level 3.
- A code-granted access level does not visually turn the physical keyswitch.
  The independent keySwitch/keyEnabled state controls its blade.
- TWINFLEXpro² has four separate alarm keys left-to-right: Silence alarms,
  Reset system, Sound alarms, Silence buzzer. There is no combined invented
  Silence/Resound key. Controls Enabled indicator and key are separate.
- Its lower-left keypad flap is independent of the right-hand access cover.
  Closing the keypad flap removes the hidden keys from the tab order. Only
  removing the right cover displays the original Write Enable switch detail.
  The right-hand interior is deliberately represented as a labelled source
  detail rather than an invented full board layout.
- TWINFLEXpro² keeps five navigation keys plus digits, * / Esc and # / left
  arrow. Its eight red zone LEDs and twelve system-indication labels remain
  in source order. Four display rows are used; XFP has two.
- Flashing LEDs honour reduced-motion preference with a static outline;
  controls-enabled slow/fast states have separate CSS rates when supplied
  by the engine.

variant-specific zone count and source images, exact key sets and order,
