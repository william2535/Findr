# FINDR visual panel assets

Seven manufacturer-sourced, locally downloaded, visually inspected assets are referenced in panels.json. Source URLs and exact MANUALARCHIVES record IDs are retained in each entry. Based on current repo clone /workspace/scratch/37b9497fd486/manualarchives-current. All record IDs were checked.

Gent: official Honeywell download centre explicitly offers product image downloads. Use the five referenced fronts, not the unused close-up or generic Vigilon image. The generic Vigilon-Panel.jpg actually depicts a Compact (visible on its LCD), so it is deliberately excluded.

Advanced: image from the current MxPro 5 product page. Historical catalogue manuals may not apply to the current generation.

Protec: product photo from official 6500 product page.

Kentec and C-TEC official pages could be read with search, but local HTML/PDF retrieval returned anti-bot interstitials, so no false image entries were created. Brochure/data-sheet .pdf files in this research directory are failed HTML responses, not usable PDFs; they must not be copied into the app.

Image ownership remains with the named manufacturers. These are product identification illustrations, not evidence of exact hardware revision, protocol, firmware or manual applicability.

Public source refs for final reporting if needed: Honeywell turn4view0; Advanced turn9search0; Protec turn27view0. Root must open sources before citing.
